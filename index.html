Questions

What specifiers ask first.

More detail in our Insights, or ask the technical team directly.

What does Structa Global supply?

Structa Global supplies certified architectural building materials in six categories: façade systems, structural components, interior finishes, sustainable materials, roofing and waterproofing, and architectural hardware. Every product carries its test reports and certificates.

Where does Structa Global work?

We support projects in the USA, Canada, the UK and India. Our headquarters is in Panampilly Nagar, Kochi, with dedicated service areas in Kochi, Trivandrum, Thrissur, Kozhikode and Kottayam.

Which certifications do your products carry?

Our quality management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015. Depending on the product, materials carry CE marking with a Declaration of Performance, BIS certification to the relevant IS code, and ASTM test data. We also supply the product data used for IGBC, LEED and GRIHA credits.

How quickly do you respond to a specification request?

Within one business day. You receive product data sheets, certificates, honest lead times and an itemised quotation.

What are typical lead times?

Stocked products reach sites in Kochi and Ernakulam in 2 to 5 working days, and elsewhere in Kerala in 3 to 7. Imported standard products take 4 to 8 weeks, and custom specifications 8 to 16 weeks. We confirm exact dates at quotation.
