IGBC, LEED or GRIHA: which certification suits your project?

Structa Global Technical TeamPublished 1 April 2025Updated 19 October 2026

Key points

  • All three systems rate buildings. Materials contribute to credits.
  • IGBC: Certified from 40 points, Super Platinum from 90.
  • LEED: Certified from 40 points, Platinum from 80.
  • GRIHA: one star from 25 points, five stars from 86.

Green certification is now a baseline expectation for many institutional, commercial and public projects in Kerala. The question is which framework to use. IGBC, LEED and GRIHA share a goal but differ in scoring, administration and the projects they suit.

One point first: all three rate whole buildings, not products. A material cannot be "IGBC certified". It can contribute to credits, and the supplier's job is to provide the evidence those credits require.

IGBC: Indian Green Building Council

IGBC was set up by the Confederation of Indian Industry and is the most widely used rating system in India. It covers new buildings, existing buildings, homes, factories, townships and campuses. Its credit categories include Sustainable Architecture and Design, Site Selection and Planning, Water Conservation, Energy Efficiency, Building Materials and Resources, and Indoor Environmental Quality.

IGBC Green New Buildings levels

LevelPoints
Certified40 to 49
Silver50 to 59
Gold60 to 74
Platinum75 to 89
Super Platinum90 to 100

Best suited to most private commercial, residential, institutional, hospitality and mixed-use projects in India, with a strong regional network of consultants.

LEED: Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design

LEED is administered by the US Green Building Council and is used in more than 180 countries and territories. In India it is common on IT parks, corporate campuses and buildings for multinational occupiers. It is also the standard framework for our projects in the USA and Canada.

LEED levels

LevelPoints
Certified40 to 49
Silver50 to 59
Gold60 to 79
Platinum80 and above

Materials credits reward Environmental Product Declarations, responsible sourcing of raw materials, material ingredient reporting and low-emitting materials. We supply EPDs, ingredient disclosures and VOC test data for the products we specify.

GRIHA: Green Rating for Integrated Habitat Assessment

GRIHA is India's national rating system, developed by TERI with the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy. It is widely required on central and state government projects, and public authorities offer incentives such as faster approvals and additional floor area for rated buildings. GRIHA scores out of 100 points plus 5 innovation points.

GRIHA star ratings

RatingPoints
1 star25 to 40
2 stars41 to 55
3 stars56 to 70
4 stars71 to 85
5 stars86 and above

Best suited to government offices, hospitals, schools, universities and public-private partnership projects.

Choose the framework your client, tenant or authority will recognise, then specify to its credits.

How to choose

  • Government or public sector: GRIHA, and check the brief for a required star rating.
  • Multinational occupier, IT park or international investor: LEED, for its global recognition.
  • Private commercial, residential, hospitality or institutional: IGBC, usually the most practical route in India.
  • Large mixed-use or campus developments: consider dual certification, typically IGBC with LEED.

Frequently asked questions

Can a building material be IGBC or LEED certified?

No. IGBC, LEED and GRIHA certify buildings. Materials contribute to credits, supported by evidence such as Environmental Product Declarations, recycled-content data and VOC test reports.

What are the IGBC Green New Buildings certification levels?

Certified 40 to 49 points, Silver 50 to 59, Gold 60 to 74, Platinum 75 to 89 and Super Platinum 90 to 100.

How many points are needed for a GRIHA 5-star rating?

86 points or more. The GRIHA thresholds are 25 to 40 for one star, 41 to 55 for two, 56 to 70 for three, 71 to 85 for four and 86 and above for five.
