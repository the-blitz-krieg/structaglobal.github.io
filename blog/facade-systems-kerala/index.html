How to specify façade systems for Kerala's coastal climate

Structa Global Technical TeamPublished 1 May 2025Updated 19 October 2026

Key points

  • Within 1 to 5 km of the coast, design for chloride corrosion first.
  • Grade 316 stainless fixings as a minimum. Avoid Grade 304 near the sea.
  • Marine-grade anodising (25 microns+) or PVDF to AAMA 2605.
  • Require CE marking to EN 13830 and independent air, water and structural tests.

Kerala's coastline runs for around 580 kilometres, and much of the state's new construction sits within reach of its salt air. Sea winds carry chlorides inland, the south-west monsoon brings sustained wind-driven rain, and humidity stays high all year. For a façade, these are the design conditions, not edge cases.

This guide sets out what we check when specifying façade systems for Kochi, Kozhikode, Trivandrum, Thrissur and Kottayam, drawing on two decades of supplying façades across the state.

Why inland specifications fall short on the coast

A system that performs well in Bengaluru can deteriorate quickly in Kochi. Four conditions account for most failures:

  • Chloride corrosion. Salt carried on sea winds attacks exposed metals, especially at joints, fixings and cut edges. Within roughly 1 to 5 kilometres of the shoreline it is a primary design consideration.
  • Monsoon wind and rain. Systems must be designed for the wind pressure at the site and height, and tested for water tightness under that pressure.
  • Thermal movement. High solar gain and daily temperature swings make the façade expand and contract. Joints and sealants must absorb that movement without losing their seal.
  • Biological growth. Persistent humidity encourages algae, moss and mould, particularly on shaded elevations.

Coastal specification is a discipline of its own, and it starts with material selection.

Material selection for coastal Kerala

Aluminium framing and coatings

Correctly specified aluminium remains the most reliable framing material for coastal façades. The protection matters as much as the alloy. For sites near the sea, specify either anodising to at least 25 microns (marine grade) or a PVDF coating tested to AAMA 2605 or QUALICOAT Class 3. Ask for the coating applicator's certification, not only the system manufacturer's.

Stainless steel fixings

Exposed fixings, anchors and ironmongery on coastal façades should be Grade 316 stainless steel as a minimum, with 316L or duplex grades for the most exposed positions. Avoid Grade 304 within 2 kilometres of the coast: its lower chloride resistance shortens service life significantly.

Glass

Solar-control glass with a low-E coating reduces cooling loads while keeping visible light transmission high. Double-glazed units with warm-edge spacers give the best balance of thermal performance and durability. In India, float glass should comply with IS 14900.

Sealants

Neutral-cure silicone sealants offer better UV resistance, temperature stability and adhesion than acetoxy-cure alternatives in this climate. Confirm compatibility with every adjacent material, and check that the sealant's movement capability exceeds the calculated movement of the joint.

Certifications to require

  • CE marking to EN 13830, the European product standard for curtain walling, with a Declaration of Performance covering structural, air, water and thermal characteristics.
  • BIS compliance for products sold in India, for example IS 733 for wrought aluminium sections and IS 14900 for float glass, with mill certificates traceable to the alloy or batch.
  • Independent air, water and structural test reports to CWCT or AAMA methods, at pressures matching your design wind load.
  • Green building documentation for IGBC or LEED projects: thermal performance data, recycled content and Environmental Product Declarations.

Engage early

The façade projects that run most smoothly share one trait: the supplier was involved at concept design. At that stage we can help define performance criteria and identify suitable systems before the design is fixed. Late changes to a façade specification are expensive, slow and usually compromise the design intent.

Frequently asked questions

Which stainless steel grade should be used for façade fixings in coastal Kerala?

Use Grade 316 stainless steel as a minimum for exposed fixings, with 316L or duplex grades for highly exposed positions. Avoid Grade 304 within about 2 km of the coast.

What coating should aluminium façades have near the sea?

Marine-grade anodising of at least 25 microns, or a PVDF coating tested to AAMA 2605 or QUALICOAT Class 3, applied by a certified applicator.

Which standard covers curtain walling for CE marking?

EN 13830 is the European product standard for curtain walling. A CE-marked system should come with a Declaration of Performance to that standard.
