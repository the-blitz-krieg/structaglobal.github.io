Quality assurance

Four stages before anything reaches site.

No product enters the portfolio, and no order leaves the factory, without clearing each stage.

  1. 01

    Manufacturer qualification

    We audit the manufacturer's quality system, production process, certification status and export record before any product is listed.

  2. 02

    Independent testing

    Claimed performance is checked against third-party laboratory reports. A manufacturer's datasheet alone is never enough.

  3. 03

    Pre-shipment inspection

    Major orders are inspected at the factory for conformity, quantity, packaging and completeness of documents.

  4. 04

    Delivery and documentation

    Every delivery arrives with its certification pack, so traceability is complete for your project records.

The documentation pack

What arrives with every delivery.

Need a document before you order? We send certificates with every quotation.

  • Material test reports from accredited laboratories
  • Mill certificates with heat or batch traceability
  • CE Declaration of Performance (DoP)
  • BIS certificates and IS code compliance letters
  • IGBC, LEED and GRIHA product contribution data
  • Safety data sheets (SDS)
  • Manufacturer warranty documentation
