Our story

Rooted in Kochi. Built for the World.

Structa Global began with one conviction: a fast-growing construction market deserved a supplier that understood both international quality standards and the conditions of the site. Twenty years later, that conviction is how we work in four markets.

The beginning

A supplier that understood the site.

In 2004, South India was building at pace. What it lacked was a supplier who combined certified international products with knowledge of local climate, codes and site realities.

We started in Kochi by building relationships on both sides: with manufacturers who would open their quality systems to audit, and with the architects, contractors and developers shaping Kerala's skyline. We learned the salt air of Kochi and Kozhikode, the monsoon intensity of Thrissur, the thermal load of Trivandrum and the humidity of Kottayam's backwaters.

That knowledge became the method. Specify to the site, document everything and stay until the building performs. It is the same method we now apply to projects in the USA, Canada and the UK.

Milestones

Two decades of Engineered Trust.

  1. 2004

    Founded in Kochi

    Structa Global opens in Panampilly Nagar, Kochi, to bring internationally certified architectural materials to South India's construction sector.

  2. 2007

    First international partnerships

    Supply agreements with certified manufacturers in Europe and Asia set the documentation standard we still apply to every product.

  3. 2010

    Trivandrum and Thrissur

    Dedicated teams in two more Kerala cities shorten specification support and delivery times for design teams statewide.

  4. 2014

    One hundred projects

    Our hundredth project, a residential tower in Kochi, marks a decade of façade and structural supply.

  5. 2017

    Green building division

    A dedicated sustainability portfolio and specification service for IGBC, LEED and GRIHA projects.

  6. 2020

    Kozhikode and Kottayam

    Two further service areas complete our five-city Kerala network.

  7. 2024

    Twenty years

    More than 500 projects supplied and over 200 certified products in the portfolio.

  8. 2026

    A new identity

    Structa Global introduces a new brand and a four-market footprint: the USA, Canada, the UK and India.

Looking ahead

The next chapter.

We are growing the sustainable share of our portfolio to at least 50% by 2027, deepening our technical consulting and extending the same standard of documentation to every market we serve.
