Local intelligence

What Kochi asks of a specification.

Three considerations we raise on almost every project here.

  1. 01

    Chloride exposure

    Within roughly 1 to 5 km of the shoreline, specify Grade 316 stainless fixings as a minimum and marine-grade anodising or high-performance PVDF on aluminium.

  2. 02

    Monsoon water ingress

    Façades should be tested for water tightness under pressure (CWCT or AAMA methods) at the design wind pressure for the site and height.

  3. 03

    Biological growth

    Persistent humidity encourages algae on shaded elevations. Choose self-cleaning or low-porosity finishes and specify a maintenance regime.
