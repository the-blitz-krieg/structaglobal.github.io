Questions

Green building, answered.

Are IGBC, LEED and GRIHA product certifications?

No. IGBC, LEED and GRIHA rate whole buildings, not individual products. Materials contribute to credits, and we supply the evidence each credit asks for: Environmental Product Declarations, recycled-content and regional-sourcing data, VOC test reports, FSC chain-of-custody certificates and thermal performance data.

What share of the Structa Global portfolio is eco-certified?

Around 40% of our products currently carry a recognised environmental certification or declaration. Our commitment is to reach at least 50% by 2027.

Do you supply FSC-certified timber?

Yes. All timber and wood products in our portfolio carry FSC certification with chain-of-custody documentation.
